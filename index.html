Why Fischbowl

A fishbowl puts everything on show. That's how we see good storytelling: get close, look properly, and let the audience see what really matters.

Fischbowl is led by Ulrich Fisch, who has spent more than five years producing Afrikaans film and video. We work with a trusted network of crew, so every project gets the right people.

We are also building Stroom, a streaming home for Afrikaans film, series and culture.
